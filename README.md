Reversed is a card game I'm making because I want to learn unity

Press the deck buttons to see the full hand of that player, remaining deck of that player, and their backlog

---

(8 cards is the cap a hand can be worth)

On your turn, you can play a # of a kind or a straight (straights are 3-5 in length, if all one suit, 2X card bonus)

In the card pool, cards your opponent are calculated like this:

2-10 : 1 card

J, Q, K : 2 cards

A : 3 cards/give 2 cards

JK : give 1 card (joker can be played as any card but is always give 1 card)

(giving cards is transferring cards from your hand to your opponent's hand)


If you can continue the hand your opponent played, you can add on your addition and spin the pool back on your opponent, so your opponent has to draw them, not you
