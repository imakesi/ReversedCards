Scriptable objects for stuff

Properties:
- Number (1-10, 1=Ace, J-Jack, Q-Queen, K-King)
- Suit (1,2,3,4 - Hearts,Diamonds,Spades,Clubs)
- Value (1,2,3,4,5 - (4=Ace give 2, 5=Joker give 1 (not in game yet)) (1-3 draw cards))

How to play:

Start with 5 cards in each players hand
(you can see each others cards)

When it's your turn you can play:
- 1 or more cards of the same number
- a straight over 3 cards up or down

If you play a straight of cards where all the cards are the same suit, you get x1.5 value, round up
(if you play 4 hearts, 5 hearts, and 6 hearts its value is 5 not 3, and 4-7 hearts )
Each play is capped at 8 cards value
and each straight is capped at 5 cards long

Aces can either be you give your opponent 2 of your cards or your opponent draws 3 new cards
2-10 opponent draws 1 card
Royals opponent draws 2

You can also play a queen, jack, and king for your opponent to draw 7 cards but you can only play that one per player per game
(if you play it you can't do it again, also royals don't get the 2x same suit straight bonus)

The reversal gimmick is basically if you play 2 3s, and your opponent plays a 3, that turn is directed at you and you draw 3 cards
reversing a straight is similar
you can continue the straight (doesn't work for QJK pair) and reverse it onto your opponent
(if your opponent plays 4-6 straight, if you have a 7, you can reverse it on them)

Jokers (not in current version because assets don't have them)
Jokers can act as any card and can continue any straight (not QJK) or reverse any card
Even though it can reverse anything non-QJK, it only is worth giving your opponent 1 of your cards

goal is to run out of cards
